Toolchest

Power tools for your Mac, in the menu bar. Every one switches on and off on its own.

Download View on GitHub

What it does

What macOS leaves out, in one menu bar app. Everything starts off, and you switch on only what you want.

Finder

Cut and paste files, Return to open, reopen closed windows, new files, Name by Content.

Windows and Dock

Quit guard for Command-Q, isolate a window, smarter Dock clicks, a green button that fills the screen.

Keyboard

Caps Lock as a Hyper key, app hotkeys, drop-down windows and a Shortcut Inspector.

Screen

Capture Text, Capture and Translate, Capture and Summarize, QR codes and a color picker.

Audio

A mic mute you can reach from anywhere, device switching with a shortcut, and a volume cap after wake.

Utilities

Keep Awake, Cleaning Mode, Dark Mode and notification shortcuts, in Shortcuts and widgets too.

Install

Free and open source, for macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gabrimatic/toolchest/main/install.sh | bash

It checks the download's SHA-256 before it installs anything. Or download the disk image and see First Open: release builds are signed ad hoc, not notarized.

Privacy

Toolchest makes no network calls and has no accounts or analytics. It never records keystrokes or clipboard contents, and asks only for what the modules you turn on need.
